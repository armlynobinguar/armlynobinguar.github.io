Something I'm building

Doqtri is a project of mine about a common problem: plans say one thing and what actually ships is another, and there's no easy way to show the difference.

You write a plan in markdown, its headings turn into a mindmap, and each item can be linked to the pull request that shipped it. Every version gets anchored on Stellar, so anyone can check what was planned and when it was done, without having to trust me.

Signing up doesn't need a crypto wallet extension; an email and a passkey on your phone are enough. Fifty people tested it on testnet and twenty on mainnet, and everything they reported has since been fixed. It's in open beta now.
